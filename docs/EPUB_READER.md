# 📖 EPUB Reader

Turn your power dashboard into a distraction-free e-reader.

## Features

- **Format Support**: Reads standard `.epub` files directly from the SD card.
- **Font Options**: Choose between **Medium** and **Large** font sizes for comfortable reading on the E-Ink display.
- **Page Saving**: Automatically remembers exactly where you left off in each book.
- **Gesture Controls**: Swipe or tap to turn pages.
- **Paper-Like Experience**: Optimized E-Ink refresh for crisp text without eyestrain.

## Setup

1.  **Prepare SD Card**: Ensure your M5Paper S3 has a FAT32 formatted SD card inserted.
2.  **Create Directory**: Create a folder named `books` on the root of the SD card.
3.  **Add Books**: Copy your `.epub` files into the `/books` folder.
    *   *Note: Filenames should be simple and avoid special characters if possible.*

## Usage

### Opening a Book
1.  Tap the **READ** button on the bottom menu bar.
2.  You will see a file browser listing all found EPUBs.
3.  Tap a book title to open it.

### Reading Controls
- **Next Page**: Tap the **Right** side of the screen or Swipe **Left**.
- **Previous Page**: Tap the **Left** side of the screen or Swipe **Right**.
- **Menu**: Tap the **MENU** button in the top-right corner to open settings.

### Reader Menu Options
- **Font Size**: Toggle between **Medium** and **Large** text.
- **Exit**: Returns to the file browser (saves your progress automatically).

## Performance Notes
- **Loading Time**: Large books may take a few seconds to load initially as the device parses the chapter structure.
- **Images**: Basic image support is included, but complex layouts may be simplified for the E-Ink display.
